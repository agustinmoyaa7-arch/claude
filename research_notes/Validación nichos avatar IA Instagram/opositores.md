# Niche validation: "Opositores" (Spain) + LatAm equivalents (concursos públicos / concurseiros) for a faceless/AI-avatar Instagram funnel selling digital study products

> Research note: Most data comes from search-result snippets. Direct page fetches failed because these hosts are not on the session's allowed network list: blog.opositatest.com, xataka.com, cope.es, appf.edu.es, elperiodic.com. Many figures come from academias and test platforms that have a commercial interest in showing a large market, and they are flagged as such. Current date: November 2026.

## 1. Market size (Spain): how many people prepare oposiciones, preparation time, spending

### Takeaway
The pool is very large. Surveys put people who are opositando or recently opositaron in the millions (about 6.8M in OpositaTest's 2025 study; 20% of adults aged 18-65 in the GAD/7 Editores 2025 survey). Hard data per exam backs this up: the Auxiliar Administrativo del Estado alone had about 47-50k admitted for about 1,700 places in May 2026, and Policía Nacional gets about 28-40k applicants a year. The state OEP has set a record for new-entry places five years running (27,697 in 2025). Typical preparation lasts 1-2 years, and academias cost about €60-300 a month. So the audience is big, long-lived and already used to paying.

### Cited Findings
**Overall pool (surveys, not official census; definitions differ):**
- OpositaTest study "El peso del opositor en España 2025" (July 2025): about 6.8 million people opositan or have opositado recently, and another 5,386,328 plan to opositar soon. Sample: 2,000 residents aged 18-55 (a population of about 23M). Source has a commercial interest. — [OpositaTest blog](https://blog.opositatest.com/estudio-peso-opositor-espana-2025/); echoed by [OKDiario](https://okdiario.com/economia/empleo/cifras-no-mienten-47-adultos-espanoles-opositan-o-plantean-ser-opositores-segun-estudio-2025-17769329) ("47% of Spanish adults opositan or are considering it")
- GAD / 7 Editores survey (Sept 2025): 20% of adults aged 18-65 are currently preparing an oposición, and 27% are considering it. — [COPE](https://www.cope.es/actualidad/economia/noticias/dos-10-espanoles-adultos-preparando-oposicion-20250915_3213904.html)
- About 52% of the active/working population says it is considering opositar, with Extremadura and Murcia above 43%. — [Xataka](https://www.xataka.com/empresas-y-economia/52-poblacion-activa-espana-esta-pensando-opositar-mayoria-procede-comunidades-paro); [Alcer](https://alcer.org/empleo/el-52-de-las-personas-trabajadoras-en-espana-se-plantea-opositar/)
- An older estimate: "close to 7 million citizens are potential opositores". — [EmpleoPublico.org](https://www.empleopublico.org/noticias/7-millones-de-potenciales-opositores/)
- An OpositaTest poll reported by Genbeta: 6 in 10 Spaniards would rather be a funcionario than have a permanent private contract or their own business. Gen Z is framed as "depressed at work → solution: opositar". — [Genbeta](https://www.genbeta.com/a-fondo/generacion-z-espanola-se-siente-muy-deprimida-trabajo-solucion-que-buscan-simple-opositar)

**Places offered (official):**

A record 27,697 are new-entry places (the 5th record year in a row), plus 8,891 internal promotion. — [Ministerio para la Transformación Digital y de la Función Pública press release](https://digital.gob.es/comunicacion/notas-prensa/secretaria-estado-funcion-publica/2025/07/2025-07-15); [INAP La Administración al Día](https://laadministracionaldia.inap.es/noticia.asp?id=1517665)

**Applicants per flagship exam:**
- Auxiliar Administrativo del Estado (OEP 2025 call, exam 23 May 2026): about 46,798-50,120 admitted on the open-entry track, and about 22,412-23,765 actually sat the exam (about 47%). Places are reported as 1,700, 1,570 or 1,544 depending on the source. Ratio is about 13-14 per place. The OEP 2026 call was still pending in the BOE as of Oct 2026, with a deadline of 31 Dec 2026. — [OpoRuta](https://oporuta.es/blog/plazas-auxiliar-administrativo-2026); [AdministracionDeJusticia.com](https://www.administraciondejusticia.com/blog/oposiciones-estado/cuanta-gente-se-presenta-auxiliar-administrativo-del-estado); [Formacion.ninja (AGE participation data 2019-2026)](https://formacion.ninja/blog/participacion-oposiciones-age); [Polaris](https://www.polarisoposiciones.com/blog/convocatoria-auxiliar-administrativo-estado-2026)

For 2025 there were about 28,500 applications (ratio about 10.3) for 2,914 places, though Campus Training says the Ministry did not publish 2025 stats. Registrations can top 70k, but most registrants don't show up. — [Campus Training](https://www.campustraining.es/noticias/cuantos-opositores-presentan-policia-nacional/); [De-Pol](https://de-pol.es/2025/10/14/ratio-plazas-policia-nacional-2025/); [Futurecops](https://academiafuturecops.es/nota-de-corte-policia-nacional-2025/); [TuOposicion](https://www.tuoposicion.com/blog/cuantos-se-presenta-policia-nacional-ratio-aspirantes-2026)

**Preparation time and spending:**
- A typical oposición takes 1-2 years to prepare; another guide uses 12-18 month scenarios. — [PreparaOposiciones](https://www.preparaoposiciones.com/blog/guias/cuanto-cuesta-preparar-oposiciones-precios-2026/); [RedOpositor](https://redopositor.com/blog/es-cuanto-cuesta-opositar-tasas-material-tiempo-2026/)
- Average spend is about €78 a month, from €59 (group C2) to €129 (group A1), per "sector surveys" with unclear methodology. — [BuscoUni](https://buscouni.com/guia/coste-real-de-opositar)
- Online academia: €60-140 a month; in-person: €100-220 a month. Administración €60-160 a month, Educación €120-220, group A academias €200-250. — [Oposiciones.es](https://oposiciones.es/noticias/precio-academia-oposiciones/); [Laboralia](https://laboraliaformacion.es/cuanto-cuesta-opositar/)
- Group A2: €100-180 a month online and €150-250 in person. Group A1: €120-220 online and €180-300 in person. Courses in the €1,500-2,500 range also exist. — [PreparaOposiciones comparativa](https://preparaoposiciones.com/blog/articulos/mejores-academias-de-oposiciones-online-2026-comparativa/); [Aprendemas](https://www.aprendemas.com/es/oposiciones/lugo/16)
- Top-tier oposiciones (Notarías, Registros) need an individual preparador, which pushes cost up. — [Campus Training](https://www.campustraining.es/noticias/cuanto-cuesta-academia-oposiciones/); [AdministracionDeJusticia (preparador cost guide 2026)](https://www.administraciondejusticia.com/blog/noticias-opos/cuanto-cuesta-un-preparador-de-oposiciones)
- Psychologist or coach: €50-80 per session (generic budget guide, not specific to opositores). — [RedOpositor](https://redopositor.com/blog/es-cuanto-cuesta-preparar-oposiciones-presupuesto-2026/)

### Inferences
- Even taking the most conservative reading (hundreds of thousands of people actively sitting exams each year, not 6.8M), the reachable audience for a Spanish-language Instagram account is very large. A single exam (Auxiliar AGE) gives about 50k admitted candidates, all in one predictable cycle.
- A 1-2 year preparation horizon at €60-300 a month means opositores are already used to spending €700-3,000+ a year. A €10-50 one-off planner or method product is an impulse purchase by comparison, so low price is not the obstacle.
- Fewer than half of admitted candidates actually sit the Auxiliar AGE exam (about 47%), and many Policía registrants never show up. That is strong indirect evidence of the "constancia / abandonment" pain the product would target.

### Gaps
- No official census of opositores exists. The survey figures (6.8M, 20% of adults) almost certainly overstate active, serious opositores, and the sources have commercial bias.
- No independent (non-academia) data on average preparation time or spend. The €78/month average has no visible methodology.
- No combined count of applicants across all regional and local convocatorias. Couldn't verify a 2024-2026 "record applicants" headline from an official source (e.g., INAP press release).

## 2. Social signals: hashtags, influencers, forums, recurring pains

### Takeaway
"Opostagram" is an established, female-skewed community on Instagram and TikTok. #opositora has about 155k Instagram posts, #opositor about 107M TikTok views, and dozens of related tags exist (#opovida, #opoage, #opocompi, #oposiciones). Human creators have mid-sized followings (about 10k-50k), which suggests the space is active but not dominated by mega-accounts. Recurring pains are burnout, anxiety, loneliness, consistency and organisation, but rigorous prevalence data is missing.

- Leading creators sit around 10k-50k followers, so a new account can realistically become a top-10 voice. That suggests a low ceiling for winner-take-all saturation, but also a moderate total attention pool on Instagram specifically. TikTok reach looks bigger (100M+ views on one tag).
- The emotional and discipline angle (soledad, ansiedad, constancia) seems less covered by academias, which mostly sell syllabus content. A psychologist-led TikTok with only about 10k followers suggests this angle is underserved.

- r/oposiciones size and activity could not be verified. Forocoches threads not checked.

## 3. Competition: academias, test apps, planner/Notion sellers, saturation

### Takeaway
The core study-content market (syllabi, tests, courses) is crowded and professionalised: OpositaTest, MAD, Adams, PreparaOposiciones, CEF, GAD, plus hundreds of local academias. The planning and organisation layer is thinner but is filling in. OpositaTest gives away a free "opotracker" for vueltas, RedOpositor sells a €49 "Planificador Pro", and small Gumroad and Etsy sellers offer €5-30 templates. A study-method or motivation product would compete mainly with free academia content and a handful of indie sellers, not with the large academias.

### Cited Findings
- OpositaTest pricing (official blog, 2026): annual plan €7.99 a month (single payment), semiannual €11.99 a month, monthly €15.99 a month. Free account available. Comprehensive courses: €845 for Auxiliar Administrativo del Estado (12 months) and €295 for several regional courses. A third-party comparison quotes €7.99 a month (= €95.88 a year). — [OpositaTest pricing guide](https://blog.opositatest.com/cuanto-cuesta-opositatest-precio-test-oposiciones); [OpositaTest plans](https://blog.opositatest.com/planes-precios-opositatest-dudas); [RedOpositor comparativa plataformas test 2026](https://redopositor.com/blog/es-comparativa-plataformas-test-oposiciones-2026/)
- OpositaTest app on the App Store (auto-renewing subscription). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/opositatest-test-oposiciones/id1182367247)
- OpositaTest already publishes free planning tools: a free "opotracker" that works as a vueltas or arrastre template, and content on "what is a planificador de oposiciones". It also promotes a branded "Método OpositaTest" (2026). — [OpositaTest opotracker](https://blog.opositatest.com/que-es-opotracker-como-se-usa); [OpositaTest planificador](https://blog.opositatest.com/planificador-oposiciones-que-es); [Método OpositaTest](https://blog.opositatest.com/?p=102882)

- Indie Notion and Gumroad sellers: RindeOpos (Notion "Starter Pack" to organise the temario, track real study hours and simulacros; 7-day kickstart kit; price not visible); Macrodemia generic study template, €5; "teacherpati", Notion for teachers and opositores, €15. — [RindeOpos Gumroad](https://rindeopos.gumroad.com); [Macrodemia](https://macrodemia.gumroad.com/l/plantilla-de-estudio-base); [teacherpati](https://teacherpati.gumroad.com/l/notion)

### Inferences
- The saturation level is high for exam content and tests, and medium-low for method, planning and discipline products. Most planners are generic PDFs, or come from academias as free lead magnets. The most direct paid competitor found (RedOpositor, €49) is recent ("founder price"), which suggests the category is still forming.
- The biggest competitive threat is free: OpositaTest's opotracker and academias' blog and planning content. A paid product has to stand out through a system (method + planner + accountability) and a stronger emotional and identity hook, not through the template alone.
- Segmenting by exam body (Auxiliar AGE, Policía, Maestros/Secundaria, Justicia) seems to be how Etsy sellers differentiate. It also matches the exam-specific hashtags (#opoage, #oposicionessecundaria, #oposicionesguardiacivil).

### Gaps
- No public sales, user or revenue figures for OpositaTest (the 2021 Actualidad Económica PDF on Stratesys was not readable), RedOpositor or the indie sellers.
- No data on Ivoox or podcast reach for opositor content. No Hotmart listings for Spanish opositor products surfaced.

## 4. Willingness to pay: products, prices, evidence of sales

### Takeaway
Opositores clearly pay for preparation (€60-300 a month for academias, €8-16 a month for test apps, €295-845 for online courses). Digital planning products exist in a €5-50 band: Etsy PDF planners at about €8-30, Notion templates at €5-15, a premium "Planificador Pro" at €49-79. Proof of volume is indirect: one adjacent teacher planner on Etsy has 730 reviews. No hard sales data for opositor-specific planners was found.

### Inferences
- A sensible price ladder: free lead magnet (weekly planner or vueltas tracker), then a €9-19 core product (Notion or PDF planner + method guide), then a €29-49 body-specific pack or "system", then possibly a €9-15 a month community or accountability tier. The ladder anchors against academia fees (€100+ a month) and OpositaTest (€8-16 a month).
- Plain templates are being commoditised toward €5-10 and have free substitutes. Margin and conversion are more likely to come from a method ("sistema de vueltas + planificación inversa desde la fecha de examen") and an emotional and accountability layer than from the file itself.

### Gaps
- No verified sales counts for opositor-specific Etsy, Gumroad or Notion products (review counts per listing not visible; fetch blocked).
- No Spanish Hotmart or Kajabi opositor courses with prices or sales found. No data on conversion rates from Instagram to these products.

## 5. LatAm equivalent: is there a comparable opportunity outside Spain?

### Takeaway
Brazil is by far the strongest civil-service-exam market and a proven benchmark: about 9-10M+ concurseiros, a market estimated at about R$5bn, the CNU 2025 with about 761k registrations, and Gran Cursos at about R$465-550M in revenue. But it is in Portuguese, so it is a separate product, not a Spanish-language add-on. Spanish-speaking LatAm (Colombia, Mexico, Peru) has merit-based concursos, but the culture of years-long preparation is weaker and fragmented. The clearest Spanish-language pockets are teacher competitions (Peru: 162k in the Carrera Pública Magisterial; Mexico USICAMM) and Colombia's CNSC (about 18k vacancies projected for 2025).

### Cited Findings
- Brazil market: projected >R$5bn in 2025, based on >9M expected registrations and concurseiros' average spending (fees, travel, materials). The sector association Aconexa admits there is no specific revenue data for the sector. — [Revide](https://www.revide.com.br/noticias/economia/mercado-de-concursos-publicos-no-brasil-deve-crescer-e-movimentar-r-5-bilhoes-em-2025/); [Diário Induscom](https://diarioinduscom.com.br/Noticias/856268/mercado_de_concursos_no_brasil_movimenta_r$_5_bilhoes_no_ano)
- Gran Cursos Online: estimates the potential concurseiro universe at >10M people and says it serves <10% of it. Revenue R$465M in 2022 (+30%), >R$500M in 2024, R$550M projected for 2025 with BTG backing; positions itself as "Netflix dos concursos". These are company projections, not audited figures. — [Economic News Brasil](https://economicnewsbrasil.com.br/2025/06/30/gran-cursos-online/); [Portal Tela](https://www.portaltela.com/economia/2025/06/30/gran-mira-receita-de-meio-bilhao-e-planeja-expansao-como-netflix-dos-concursos/); [NeoFeed](https://neofeed.com.br/negocios/com-dinheiro-do-btg-gran-cursos-online-vai-prestar-vestibular-para-novas-areas/)
- Another market leader (Estratégia, per Exame) invested R$100M in higher education. — [Exame](https://exame.com/bussola/bussola-cia-lider-em-concursos-investe-r-100-mi-em-curso-superior/)

- Brazil shows how far the model can go (millions of concurseiros, billion-real spending, Hotmart and Notion planners already sold for concursos). One account can cover Spain plus LatAm only by moving to broader "estudiar para un examen largo / disciplina de estudio" positioning, which dilutes the niche identity. ## 6. Risks: seasonality tied to convocatorias; credibility of a fictional avatar

### Takeaway
Seasonality is real but less sharp than it looks. OEPs are approved yearly (the state OEP around July), calls and exams for the many bodies (AGE, Policía, Guardia Civil, Educación, Justicia, regional and local) are spread through the year, and preparation lasts 1-2 years, so demand is fairly continuous with spikes around OEP approval, calls and exam dates. The bigger risk is trust. Opositores are emotionally vulnerable and buy partly on a "this person passed / is in it with me" story. Presenting a fictional avatar as a real opositora who passed would be misleading and potentially reputationally damaging. No direct data on audience acceptance of AI avatars in this niche was found.

### Cited Findings
- The state OEP is approved around mid-year (OEP 2025 approved 15 July 2025); the OEP 2026 Auxiliar AGE call had a BOE deadline of 31 Dec 2026; the OEP 2025 Auxiliar exam was on 23 May 2026. These dates show staggered cycles. — [Ministerio press release](https://digital.gob.es/comunicacion/notas-prensa/secretaria-estado-funcion-publica/2025/07/2025-07-15); [Polaris](https://www.polarisoposiciones.com/blog/convocatoria-auxiliar-administrativo-estado-2026); [OpoRuta](https://oporuta.es/blog/plazas-auxiliar-administrativo-2026)
- Policía Nacional and Guardia Civil follow their own annual OEP cycle. — [MAD OPE 2025 Policía/Guardia Civil](https://mad.es/noticias/672_ope-policia-nacional-guardia-civil-2025.html); [Mundopositor](https://mundopositor.info/policia-nacional-convocatoria-2025/)
- Preparation takes 1-2 years (continuous demand between calls). — [PreparaOposiciones](https://www.preparaoposiciones.com/blog/guias/cuanto-cuesta-preparar-oposiciones-precios-2026/)
- Volatility: the total OEP fell about 9% from 2024 (40,146) to 2025 (about 36,600), and unions did not back it, so offer size depends on politics and budgets. — [Legal Today](https://www.legaltoday.com/actualidad-juridica/noticias-de-derecho/el-gobierno-aprueba-36-500-plazas-para-la-oep-de-2025-casi-un-9-menos-que-el-ano-anterior-2025-07-15/); [Menorca.info](https://www.menorca.info/actualidad/nacional/2025/07/15/2430411/gobierno-aprueba-oferta-empleo-publico-36600-plazas-inferior-2024.html)
- Credibility precedent: a successful opositora influencer ("Laura") uses an illustrated alter ego for part of her content while being a real opositora. The leading creators listed (Srta Opositora, Opositora en Apuros, Mente Opositora) all base their authority on real lived experience or professional credentials (psychologist). — [Mundopositor](https://mundopositor.info/como-ser-influencer-de-oposiciones-los-7-opositores-mas-seguidos/); [TikTok @mente.opositora](https://www.tiktok.com/@mente.opositora)

### Inferences
- Seasonality: plan content and launches around OEP approval (summer), each body's call (BOE), the start of the academic year (Sept, "new year, new temario"), January resolutions, and the 4-8 weeks before big exams (anxiety and simulacros content). Diversifying across bodies smooths demand.
- Avatar credibility: the safer positioning is an openly fictional or illustrated character (a mascot or "compañera de estudio") or a faceless "sistema/método" brand, not a fake story about passing an exam. Claims like "I passed with this method" made by an AI persona would be deceptive. In Spain they may also run into consumer-protection and advertising rules (Ley General de Publicidad, EU AI Act transparency duties for AI-generated content), though this wasn't researched here. Testimonials must come from real buyers.
- The emotional pains (ansiedad, soledad) raise the ethical bar: an AI persona offering "support" should avoid posing as a psychologist and should point to professional help.

- No month-by-month search-interest data (Google Trends for "oposiciones", "planificador oposiciones") could be retrieved to quantify seasonality.
- Spanish and EU legal requirements for labelling AI influencers in advertising were not researched in this pass.
